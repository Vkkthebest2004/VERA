## 🎙️ Recording Tips for the Speaker
1. **Pace Yourself:** Do not rush. Let the screen action complete before transitioning sentences.
2. **Key Terms Pronunciation:**
   - **VERA:** *"VEER-uh"*
   - **Artha:** *"AR-thuh"*
   - **ROCE:** *"R-O-C-E"* (or *"Return on Capital Employed"*)
   - **SEBI LODR:** *"SEB-ee L-O-D-R"*
   - **BSE / NSE:** *"B-S-E and N-S-E"*
3. **Tone:** Crisp, authoritative, modern, and enthusiastic.
