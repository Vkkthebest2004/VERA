## 🎤 Recording Hacks for You:
1. **Pacing:** Har scene ke baad 1 second ka gap hai, isliye bilkul aaram se bolein.
2. **Energy:** Pehli line mein thoda excitement rakhein, jaise aap kisi dost ko ek kamaal ki nayi cheez dikha rahe hain!
3. **Mistake ho toh:** Video stop mat karo, bas 2 second ruko aur dobara woh line bol do.
