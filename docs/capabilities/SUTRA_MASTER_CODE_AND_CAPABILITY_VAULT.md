## 🏛️ 1. The Core Philosophy & 6 Invariant Principles

SUTRA is an evidence-first financial claim verification engine built around the **SANGYAN Investor-Resilience Journey**:
$$\text{INPUT} \longrightarrow \text{UNDERSTAND} \longrightarrow \text{DECOMPOSE} \longrightarrow \text{INVESTIGATE} \longrightarrow \text{VERIFY} \longrightarrow \text{TRACE EVIDENCE} \longrightarrow \text{EXPLAIN} \longrightarrow \text{PROTECT} \longrightarrow \text{USER DECIDES}$$

### The 6 Absolute Invariant Principles (Never Violate):

1. **🔒 Invariant 1: Multi-Modal Ingestion Fidelity**
   - Text, PDF documents, image screenshots, and audio voice notes must all be parsed using native, high-fidelity engines.
   - Images use native **Apple Vision Neural Engine OCR** (`scripts/sutra-ocr`) + **Google Gemma 3 Multimodal** (`gemma3:4b`).
   - Audio notes use **Faster-Whisper** (`base_int8` with AV 13.1.0).
   - PDFs use **PyMuPDF** (`fitz`).
   - *Never replace these with generic mock extractors.*

2. **🔒 Invariant 2: Verbatim & Human-Readable Dual Layer**
   - The user must **always** be able to inspect both:
     1. The **exact verbatim extracted text** from their document/audio (untruncated, with line numbers and copy capability).
     2. The **human-readable plain-English breakdown** generated by Google Gemma 3, decoding financial numbers (crores, basis points, YoY/QoQ percentages) and formulating exchange cross-examination questions.

3. **🔒 Invariant 3: SUTRA Principle 5 ("Absence of Evidence ≠ Proof of Falsehood")**
   - When a claim, image, or document has no matching corporate disclosure on BSE/NSE archives, the system must **NEVER** show a blank screen or a confusing "nothing found".
   - It must classify the claim as `UNSUBSTANTIATED_SPECULATION` with status `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
   - It must explicitly explain that under **SEBI (LODR) Regulations 2015 (Regulation 30)**, listed companies are legally required to report material developments within **24 hours**. The absence of such a filing indicates unverified market speculation or potential pump-and-dump activity.

4. **🔒 Invariant 4: 5-Tier Provenance & Exact Statutory Coordinates**
   - Every official statutory finding must link to a document in the hierarchy:
     - `TIER_1_REGULATORY`: BSE, NSE, SEBI, RBI, MCA statutory filings.
     - `TIER_2_COMPANY_FILING`: Audited annual reports, earnings transcripts, board resolutions.
     - `TIER_3_FINANCIAL_MEDIA`: Reuters, Bloomberg, Mint, Economic Times.
     - `TIER_4_UNVERIFIED_SOCIAL`: WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, YouTube finfluencers.
   - Every citation must provide: **Document Title**, **Filing Date**, **Page Number**, **Paragraph Number**, and **Exact Quoted Text**.

5. **🔒 Invariant 5: Numerical Variance Reconciliation Engine**
   - Claims with inflated numbers (e.g., claiming a ₹12,500 Cr order when the official filing states ₹1,250 Cr) must be systematically reconciled.
   - The engine must compute the discrepancy factor (e.g. `10x Exaggeration (Inflated by 1,000%)`) and flag the mismatch.

6. **🔒 Invariant 6: Mandatory Investor Protection & SEBI SCORES Integration**
   - Every investigation must conclude with actionable safe steps, risk classification (`EXTREME_RISK`, `HIGH_RISK`, `MODERATE`, `LOW`), and clickable direct redressal links:
     - **SEBI SCORES Grievance Portal**: [`https://scores.sebi.gov.in`](https://scores.sebi.gov.in)
     - **SEBI Intermediary Registry**: [`Verify Registered Intermediaries`](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/other/OtherAction.do?doRecognisedFpi=yes&intmId=13)

7. **🔒 Invariant 7: Plain-Language Takeaway for Everyday Investors & Social Media Crawl Audit**
   - Jargon-Free Takeaways: Every investigation result must include `plain_language_takeaway` written in simple, conversational English without technical regulatory terms, explaining whether a claim is real or an internet rumor/pump scheme.
   - Social Media Web Crawling: Autonomous cross-referencing across Instagram reels, Telegram tip channels, WhatsApp forwards, X/Twitter cashtags, and Reddit communities, auditing social origins against statutory exchanges.
   - Visual Surfing Animation: Seamless animated radar-sweep and orbital node transitions displaying live audited sources (Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp, X, Reddit, BSE, NSE, SEBI).
